## Pitfalls (the ones that bite every session; the rest are in `dev/PITFALLS.md`)
- A refusal inside `tbStudy`'s per-row setup must come before any switch is set (the `try` that restores them starts later): a throw there leaks switches into every later run. Put new refusals in the loop at the top of `tbStudy`.
- Never combine `rt.cr` with `rt.chi` 1 (8-11% unplaced); with ageing and PTH's books set `fx.pe`. Hours depend on population size in the frozen engine (C2.2: +11% at 500, +1.8% at 10,000).
- With the weighted tail (`wt`, in the main row), every decision and the price module are unweighted: a rule that charges capital income (S72's `gv.base 'all'`) lets the tail's 18 adults move hours and inflation. Read such rows without the tail (S72 Amendment 2). Debt-reading keys explode in some readings: summarise by medians and `gvY100`.
- Under the flat contribution (`fin 'tax'`, no `taxBase`), `hybrid` and the Source, never quote inflation or wealth without `fx.fin` set: with it unset the part a capped rate cannot raise is paid by no one (`finGapSh`). `fx: true` does not include `fin`.
- `o.wy` needs the year split (`pr` with `q` > 1 under the capacity rule) to differ from the published measure; with `q` 1 it equals it.
- `po` acts only under open access and refuses `pa {ops:'outside'}`. Under `po` the reported `pfCut` and `csFree` follow the reading; the subsidy is `poSub` (the PTF cut less its free part), not `cCut`, which includes PTH. Under `pay:'price'` the density tests and SZH no longer touch the PTF cut.
- `bg` needs the labour response (testbed runs set it); it throws otherwise. A bargained raise has no payer and lowers inflation under today's price rule (it adds to the income new money is divided by): never quote a reading above 0.03 for a wealth or price result without saying so.
- Unit tests that compare whole studies need an uncounted warm-up run and the same row structure each time (cached supply paths are shared by the rows of a study).
- `cr` reads adults' weights through four yearly totals (the one rule that does). Its totals are taken at the first adult's turn, not before the year's loop: a pathway exit pays equity into wealth in between. With `w: 0` the `cr*` keys are unweighted and not comparable in dollars with other rows.
- The no-debt-relief reading (`chi: 1`) is not a bound for the creditor readings: it removes the borrower's gain; `cr` adds the saver's loss. Quote both beside any wealth result, with `cr: {out: 0}` as the upper bound on creditor losses.
- Never quote an `rt` wealth or concentration result without the no-debt-relief reading (`chi: 1`) beside it: inflated-away mortgages are a gain with no loser, and the sign of the programme's effect on the top 1% share turns on it.
- With the tail (`wt`) on, read only the weighted keys (`wt*`, `dm*`, `rt*`); the unweighted Ginis and top shares are not valid. No rule may read `a._w`.
- A pilot's off rows must carry CPS earnings (`lt: {s: 'all', m: 'all'}`) to match S66's rows.
- `earlier-engine.html` must be resynced after any engine edit: `python3 dev/tools/sync_earlier.py` (domtest fails otherwise).
- Run studies from a clean worktree (a `node_modules` symlink makes the run's manifest "dirty").
- Pilot result files write 0 for keys past the run; never read that 0 as a value.
- Under any price rule, read wealth poverty and median real wealth as yearly averages (`ppov_*`, `pmedW_*`) beside year 20: the year-20 reading changes sign with the rule. The rationed share and shadow prices on a yearly step measure the step (22.5% yearly, 1.9% in quarters).
- `node domtest.js` needs `npm install` first; it fails until the lineage is proved and `earlier-engine.html` is resynced.
- The second implementation (`s50_release.py`) reproduces the old engine until a new packet is built.
