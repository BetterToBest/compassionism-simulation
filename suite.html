Beyond one poverty line

The math and code of this simulation were engineered by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, from the concepts in Duke Johnson's book Better To Best and his related vision for eradicating extreme poverty while enriching cultures and supporting human flourishing. The model has not yet been reviewed by an independent economist; the full code is open for anyone to check, and expert collaborators are welcome.

Most policy is judged by one line: how many people fall below it. This page shows what that hides. It follows the same simulated adults year by year, with Compassionism and with no program, on the six-tier ladder of the Basic Living Economic Index (BLEI) paper, and asks what their days of security are made of, who gains, who does not, and how much the answer depends on the measure and on the world they live in.
