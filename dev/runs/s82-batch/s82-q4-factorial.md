Effect: programme run minus its own no-programme run, mean over seeds; 95% paired-bootstrap interval (10000 replicates, generator seed 20261118). Shares are the backed share (the part of the payout matched by new output); share 1 is the main row (full backing, a design parameter). Preregistration: research-update `prereg/2026-11-s82-v6-supply-indexing.md` with Amendments 1-4.

The 2 x 2 x 2 design at the central readings: A, the sourced supply limit (market rule, or the design's rule); B, wages indexed 0.75 and made up a year later; C, the BU a year behind. v(S): the change the readings in S make to the programme's effect at that share, paired by seed. Alone: v(A), v(B), v(C). Interactions (Harsanyi dividends): AB = v(AB) - v(A) - v(B), and so on; ABC = v(ABC) - v(AB) - v(AC) - v(BC) + v(A) + v(B) + v(C). Joint: v(ABC). Shapley values split the joint change (they add up to it). * an interaction whose 95% interval excludes zero (claimed, research rule 4): then the readings' separate changes are not summed anywhere and the joint row is the one reported (decision rule 7). At full backing the limit is inactive, so only B x C is read there.
