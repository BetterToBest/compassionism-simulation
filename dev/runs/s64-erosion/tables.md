**Reading the tables.** *Published*: the effect as the round-2 reports print it. *Without erosion*: the same effect with each adult's programme real wealth less the erosion term, so that the adult's opening money claims (deposits, bonds and the non-stock part of funds, less floating- and fixed-rate debt) fare as in the no-programme run. *Erosion share*: the part that is erosion over the published effect; positive means erosion is part of the published effect, negative means it works against it. *All-cash bound*: the same share on S66's term, which treats all opening wealth as cash; the frozen row holds wealth by asset class, so this overstates the erosion. Intervals: 95%, paired bootstrap over seeds.
