# s148: outside audits (four angles), for free-plan chats

Prepared Oct 15, 2026. The Compassionism Simulation is an open agent-based model of Duke Johnson's framework. It has had Claude-written checks but no independent economist. This packet lets four separate chats (any AI the owner chooses, one chat each, with web access if available) audit it from four angles. The replies are pasted into one fresh chat afterwards, which sorts the findings into corrections, decisions for Duke, and optional polish (template at the end).

Attach to each chat: `ODD.md`, `MODEL_SPEC.md`, `ECONOMIST-REVIEW.md`, `ledger-open-items-oct15.md` and `next-study-audit-prompt.md` (the general description of the model and what to ask). Live page: https://bettertobest.github.io/compassionism-simulation/ · code: https://github.com/BetterToBest/compassionism-simulation · the framework's papers: https://bettertobest.github.io/research-hub/ · how Compassionism compares with other whole systems: https://bettertobest.github.io/NormativeEvaluation/

Paste one of `prompt-A` to `prompt-D` into each chat. Each prompt tells the auditor to write for a reader who is not an economist and to separate "this is wrong" from "this is a choice I would make differently".

## Sorting chat (paste the four replies after this)

You are sorting four outside audits of a simulation. For each finding give: a one-line statement; which audit raised it; whether two or more raised it; and one of **Correction** (something is wrong and can be fixed without a judgement), **Decision for Duke** (a modelling or concept choice), **Optional polish**, or **Not a problem** (with the reason). Rank corrections by how much they could change a published figure. Do not soften or drop a finding because it is awkward for the project. End with the five most important things to do first.
