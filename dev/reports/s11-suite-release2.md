# The visual suite on release 2 (S11, Nov 16, 2026)

**What changed.** The visual suite page (`suite.html`: the BLEI ladder year by year, what the days of basic living are made of, who takes part, four poverty lines side by side, and the spread across runs and environments) now opens on release 2: no programme, the C2.2 design and the release design, 500 paired runs, Reference, Adverse and Stress, 20 and 40 years. Release 1's page is unchanged and one click away (the "Release" switch, or `suite.html?rel=1`).

**Where the numbers come from.** Release 2's stored results held yearly poverty and median wealth, but not the ladder's six tiers, the median BLEI or its parts. So release 2's main row was run again (job X2, GitHub Actions, free; preregistered in research-update `prereg/2026-11-x2-series-addendum.md` before it ran), recording those series. The run used today's engine (commit 5b62481) and was checked, value by value, against release 2's stored results from engine 104691f: every engine output of every row and seed was equal (1,465,500 values per environment and horizon). So these are release 2's runs, recorded in more detail. The results are in BetterToBest/compassionism-sim-results, folder `x2-5b62481`, with `run.txt` and `SHA256SUMS`. The page reads them from `data/releases/r2/`; release 1's files (`data/releases/v5.6/`) are untouched.

**One more check.** Averaged over the years, the new series' share of adults under 30 days of basic living (same rules for both runs) reproduces release 2's published basic-living figures exactly, for every row, environment and horizon.

**How to read it.** Each figure is one row's level: the mean over 500 runs, with the 10th to 90th percentile across them. The page does not subtract one row from another. Where it states a difference (under 30 days, below the cost of living, wealth poverty, inflation), it quotes release 2's published effect with its 95% interval. Release 2's main row assumes every programme dollar is matched by new output (full backing, the design's statement; a design parameter), with today's price rule.

**What it shows (Reference, 40 years, means over the runs).** With no programme, 12.6% of adults are in Crisis in the last year and 64.7% reach Flourishing; with the C2.2 design, 0.0% and 95.8%, and the median adult covers 3,891 days of basic living against 1,537. The C2.2 design reaches every adult; under the release design fewer take part, most of all in Stress, where its gains are about a third as large. Every figure is on the page with its spread, for every environment and horizon, including Adverse and Stress.

**What it does not show.** Release 2 recorded the BLEI series only: the paper's other five measures (EDC, EPPM, FBS, CSI, IPBI) and the measures study (who moves in and out of Crisis) exist for release 1's rows only, and the release 2 view says so. Participants and non-participants are self-selected groups. The model has not been reviewed by an independent economist.
